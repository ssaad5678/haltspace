Questions

FAQ

Is Haltspace free?

Yes. Haltspace is free to download and use, with no account and no subscription.

Where are my notes stored?

On your own computer, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. Use Home → Backup to save a copy of your whole workspace as a file, and Restore backup to bring it back on any computer.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows this for apps from new publishers that haven't bought a code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is built automatically from the public source code on GitHub.

How do updates work?

When a new version is released, Haltspace shows a notice. Open Updates and click Restart to update; it downloads, installs and reopens by itself, and your notes stay where they are.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Windows app works fully offline. You only need a connection to check for updates and to load online fonts.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. A macOS app is on the roadmap. Until then you can use Haltspace in your browser on any computer.
